How to test

Hover, drag, scroll and click as you would on the live store. Product links open the real product pages on shop.ranchers.com.

The bag in Try the Look is a shortlist on this page only; nothing is ordered and no payment is taken.

The ad landing pages are built for phones first, since most ad clicks come from Reels and Stories. Open them on a phone, or narrow your browser window.

The Rail scales as one picture, so it reads best on a laptop or larger. The other concepts are built for phones too.
