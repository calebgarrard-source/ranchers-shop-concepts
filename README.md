# Texas Ranchers shop concepts

Three homepage concepts for [shop.ranchers.com](https://shop.ranchers.com), built from renders of the real merch with the club's marks, type and textures. These are test pages only: nothing on the live store has changed.

**Live pages:** https://calebgarrard-source.github.io/ranchers-shop-concepts/

| Concept | Link | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Every Season | https://calebgarrard-source.github.io/ranchers-shop-concepts/every-season/ | Load animation, wheel scroll, the shop-the-lineup grid, the marquee |
| Try the Look | https://calebgarrard-source.github.io/ranchers-shop-concepts/try-the-look/ | Drag or swipe the ring, tap a look, tap pins, pick sizes, add a full look |
| The Rail | https://calebgarrard-source.github.io/ranchers-shop-concepts/the-rail/ | Hover along the rail, click a top, use the arrows or keyboard (desktop) |

Notes for testers:
- Product links open the real product pages. The Try the Look bag is a shortlist stored in your browser; no order or payment happens here.
- The Rail scales as one picture, so it reads best on a laptop or larger.
- Body and label type (Sweet Sans Pro, Sweet Square Pro) loads from the club's Adobe Fonts kit and shows once this site's domain is allowed in that kit; until then a close stand-in appears. Headlines use Solano Gothic MVB, used under the club's web licence.
